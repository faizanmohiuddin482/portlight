A · Menu bar popover

Click the icon in the menu bar and a frosted list drops down, grouped by project. Each row has a moon to keep the Mac awake and an ✕ to quit the process. Type a port number to check whether it's free. It feels like the Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth menu.

C · Full window

A proper window like System Settings or Activity Monitor, with a sidebar of views (All, By Project, Stay Awake, On Network, History) and a sortable table. Best for digging in, but heavier for a quick check.

My guess: A as the everyday app, with C opening from "Open Ports Window…" at the bottom of the menu when you want the full picture. B is nicer to look at but hides the list one click deeper.
